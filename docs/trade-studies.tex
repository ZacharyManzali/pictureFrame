Every rating, pinout and dimension used here must come from a datasheet in \texttt{docs/datasheets/} (local only) and be cited. Unverified values are marked \textbf{unverified}.

\section{Raspberry Pi model}
Drives R-CMP-1, R-MEC-1, R-NET-1, R-UPL-*. Candidates: Zero 2 W, 4, 5 (TBD). Compare depth, RAM, USB, idle power.

\section{Display (10'' IPS, touch)}
Drives R-DSP-1..3, R-FTR-4, R-MEC-1. Candidates TBD. Check panel thickness, touch interface, backlight control, input (HDMI, DSI), supply voltage.

\section{Storage}
Drives R-STO-1, R-MEC-1. Candidates TBD: 2.5'' SSD, M.2 NVMe in USB enclosure. Check height and bus-powered current draw.

\section{Sensors}
Drives R-FTR-3, R-FTR-4. Candidates TBD: PIR module, I2C ambient light sensor.

\section{Power supply}
Drives R-PWR-1..5, R-CST-1. Candidates TBD. Universal input 100--240~VAC, NBR~14136 plug or detachable cord, INMETRO listing, output sized from the power budget plus 20\%.

\section{Enclosure}
Drives R-MEC-1..4. Candidates TBD: store-bought frame. Check inner depth and opening size against the panel.
